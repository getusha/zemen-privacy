Privacy Policy — Zemen Ethiopian Calendar

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Zemen Ethiopian Calendar ("the app") is published by Hagerly.

Summary

The app has no account and we run no servers: your reminders and settings stay on your phone. To keep the app free, it shows ads from Google AdMob, and only the ads use the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Reminders you create (title, note, date, time, repeat) and your settings (language, tradition, colour, numerals) are stored only on your phone.
  • If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy these to your own Google account so they come back when you change phones. We cannot see that backup.

Ads

The app shows Google AdMob ads on the holidays list and the day view. To show and measure ads, Google collects your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), basic device and app information and how you interact with the ads (views and taps). Google uses this under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and Settings → Privacy choices lets you change it at any time.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, which also stops personalised ads.

We do not receive or store this data ourselves, and we never sell it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your reminders and holiday alerts.
  • Exact alarms: so reminders ring at the exact time you set.
  • Run at startup: to re-arm your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for reminder alerts.
  • Internet and network state: only to load ads. The calendar, holidays, reminders and widgets all work offline.
  • Advertising ID: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, for ads as described above.

The app has no analytics of its own and no tracking beyond Google's ads.

Children

The app is meant for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
